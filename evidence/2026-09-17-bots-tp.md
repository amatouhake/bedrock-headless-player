## Live result

A temporary local identity `DemoDirector` spawned, moved away from spawn with
normal `PlayerAuthInput`, and sent the normal chat message `  BOTS TP  `. Its
client-side trigger position was `(0.749086976, 62.620010376, 11.699999809)`;
Endstone's feet-position representation was `(0.75, 61.00, 11.70)`.

BDS logged ten successful native teleports:

```text
[OwnerbotDemo] OwnerBot teleport name=OwnerBot01 success=True target=(0.75,61.00,11.70)
...
[OwnerbotDemo] OwnerBot teleport name=OwnerBot10 success=True target=(0.75,61.00,11.70)
[OwnerbotDemo] bots tp requested by DemoDirector at (0.75,61.00,11.70); teleported 10/10 connected OwnerBots
```

Every headless client independently received a server `move_player` packet with
`mode=teleport` and the same player-input coordinate
`(0.749086976, 62.620010376, 11.699999809)`. OwnerBot10 died shortly after the
first move; its normal client-driven respawn completed and the plugin's bounded
post-respawn retry sent the same server teleport again. The probe disconnected
cleanly, and the status endpoint returned ten players.

The existing `bots` behavior was then live-tested twice. Each cycle produced
ten `ready!` replies, ten jump starts, ten completions after approximately ten
seconds, and no bot client error event. This confirms the new server-side chat
listener does not consume or break the normal chat packet seen by the external
clients.
